# Fashion Retail Sales Analysis using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing fashion retail sales data using SQL to extract meaningful business insights. It covers sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional analysis, and monthly sales growth.

This project was completed as part of **Day 28 of the Data Analytics & Data Science task series**.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze total sales, total profit, orders, and customers.
- Identify top-performing product categories and regions.
- Analyze customer spending and repeat purchases.
- Identify top-selling, bottom-selling, and most profitable products.
- Rank customers, products, and product categories.
- Calculate monthly sales growth.

## 🗂️ Dataset Information

The dataset contains fashion retail sales information, including:

- **Order Details:** Order ID, Order Date, Order Channel, Payment Method
- **Customer Details:** Customer ID, Customer Name, Gender, Age, Customer Type, Segment
- **Product Details:** Product Category, Product Name, Brand, Size, Color
- **Sales Metrics:** Units Sold, Unit Price, Discount, Sales Amount, Cost Amount, Profit, Profit Margin
- **Geographical Details:** City, State, Region
- **Additional Metrics:** Return Status, Order Year, Order Quarter, Order Month, Sales Category, Profit Category, Product Performance, Order Size

## 📊 Analysis Performed

The project includes 20 SQL queries organized into the following sections:

### 1. Basic Business Analysis
- Total sales
- Total profit
- Total number of orders
- Total number of customers
- Average order value

### 2. Sales & Profit Analysis
- Sales by product category
- Sales by region
- Profit by product category
- Profit by region
- Monthly sales analysis

### 3. Customer Analysis
- Top 10 customers by spending
- Identification of repeat customers
- Average spending per customer

### 4. Product Analysis
- Top 10 products by sales
- Bottom 10 products by sales
- Top 10 most profitable products

### 5. Advanced SQL Analysis
- Customer ranking based on sales
- Product ranking based on units sold
- Monthly sales growth percentage
- Product category ranking based on sales

## 💡 SQL Concepts Used

- `SELECT` and `FROM`
- Aggregate functions: `SUM()`, `COUNT()`, `AVG()`
- `GROUP BY` and `ORDER BY`
- `HAVING`
- Subqueries
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window functions: `RANK()` and `LAG()`
- `ROUND()` and `NULLIF()`

## 📈 Expected Outcomes

This analysis helps understand overall business performance, identify high-performing products and categories, evaluate customer purchasing behavior, compare regional sales, and track monthly sales trends.

The results can support data-driven decisions related to sales strategy, product performance, and customer retention.
