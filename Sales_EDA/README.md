# Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) — Fashion Retail Sales Analysis

## 1. Overview

Sales EDA explores sales performance and profitability in the fashion retail dataset. The objective is to understand sales distribution, compare performance across categories and regions, identify leading products, and examine monthly sales patterns.

The analysis was conducted using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

## 2. Input Dataset

`Fashion_Retail_Sales_Cleaned_Transformed.csv`

## 3. Sales Analysis

### 3.1 Total Sales

Calculated total sales by summing the `Sales_Amount` column to establish an overall sales baseline.

### 3.2 Monthly Sales Analysis

Aggregated sales by month and visualized the results using a line chart.

This analysis helps examine variations in monthly sales and identify periods of relatively higher or lower sales.

### 3.3 Sales by Product Category

Aggregated sales by `Product_Category` and compared the categories using a bar chart.

The analysis helps identify which categories contribute more to overall sales.

### 3.4 Sales by Region

Grouped sales by `Region` and compared regional sales performance using a bar chart.

This comparison helps identify geographical differences in sales contribution.

### 3.5 Top Products by Sales

Aggregated sales by product name and identified the top-performing products based on total sales.

A bar chart was used to compare leading products.

### 3.6 Sales Distribution

Examined the distribution of `Sales_Amount` using a histogram to understand the spread of transaction sales values.

## 4. Profit Analysis

### 4.1 Profit by Product Category

Aggregated profit by product category to compare the contribution of different categories to total profit.

### 4.2 Regional Profitability

Compared profit across regions to identify geographical differences in profitability.

### 4.3 Monthly Profitability

Explored profit across monthly periods to examine changes in financial performance over time.

## 5. Visualizations

The notebook includes:

- Monthly Sales Line Chart
- Category Sales Bar Chart
- Region Sales Bar Chart
- Top Products Bar Chart
- Profit by Category Bar Chart
- Sales Distribution Histogram

## 6. Business Value

The analysis provides a basis for answering questions about overall sales, monthly trends, category performance, product contribution, regional performance, and profitability.

The findings can help management identify areas for further investigation and evaluate where sales and profitability improvement opportunities may exist.

## 7. Conclusion

Sales EDA provides an initial analytical view of the retail business by comparing sales and profit across different dimensions. It establishes the foundation for more detailed customer-level and product-level investigations.
