# Fashion Retail Sales Analysis – KPI Development

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on developing Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for the Fashion Retail Sales dataset using Python and Pandas. The objective is to convert sales data into meaningful business metrics that help evaluate revenue, profitability, customer activity, and monthly sales performance.

The analysis includes calculating essential business KPIs and measuring month-on-month revenue growth to understand changes in sales over time.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Calculate key business performance indicators.
- Measure total revenue and overall profitability.
- Understand order volume and customer base.
- Calculate Average Order Value (AOV) and Profit Margin.
- Compare monthly revenue with the previous month's revenue.
- Calculate average and median monthly revenue growth.
- Understand how KPIs support business performance monitoring and decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Python** – Data analysis and calculations
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation, aggregation, and KPI calculations
- **NumPy** – Numerical computing
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Writing and executing analysis code

## 🧮 KPI Formulas

### 1. Total Revenue
**Formula:** Sum of all `Sales_Amount` values.

Measures the total revenue generated by the business.

### 2. Total Profit
**Formula:** Sum of all `Profit` values.

Measures the total profit generated from sales.

### 3. Total Orders
**Formula used:** Count of records in the dataset using `Order_ID`.

Measures the number of order records included in the analysis.

### 4. Total Customers
**Formula:** Count of unique `Customer_ID` values.

Measures the number of distinct customers in the dataset.

### 5. Average Order Value (AOV)
**Formula:**

AOV = Total Revenue / Total Orders

Measures the average revenue generated per order.

### 6. Profit Margin
**Formula:**

Profit Margin (%) = (Total Profit / Total Revenue) × 100

Measures the percentage of revenue retained as profit.

### 7. Monthly Revenue Growth
**Formula:**

Growth (%) = ((Current Month Revenue − Previous Month Revenue) / Previous Month Revenue) × 100

Measures the percentage increase or decrease in revenue compared with the previous month.

The first month has no previous-month revenue available in the dataset, so its growth percentage is recorded as `NaN`.

### 8. Average and Median Monthly Growth
- **Mean:** Calculates the average of the available monthly revenue growth percentages.
- **Median:** Identifies the middle value of the monthly revenue growth percentages.

The mean and median provide different perspectives on monthly revenue changes.
