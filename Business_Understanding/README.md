# Business Understanding — Fashion Retail Sales Analysis

## 1. Overview

Business Understanding is the first stage of the Fashion Retail Sales & Customer Performance Analysis project. The purpose of this stage is to define the business problem, establish analytical objectives, identify stakeholders, and determine the business questions that need to be answered through data analysis.

## 2. Business Background

The business is a fashion retail company selling different products across multiple categories and locations. The available data includes sales, customers, products, orders, and geographical information.

Management requires a comprehensive understanding of business performance to evaluate sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, category performance, and regional trends.

## 3. Problem Statement

The company has historical sales and customer-related data, but it requires a structured analytical view to understand overall business performance.

The analysis aims to identify performance patterns, compare products and categories, understand customer contributions, evaluate profitability, and highlight areas that may require further investigation.

## 4. Business Objectives

- **Sales Performance:** Analyze sales and revenue across products, categories, locations, customer segments, and time periods.
- **Profitability:** Evaluate profit and profit margins across products, categories, customers, locations, and time periods.
- **Customer Performance:** Understand customer purchasing behavior, customer types, demographics, and customer contributions.
- **Product and Category Performance:** Identify high-performing and underperforming products based on sales, quantity, revenue, and profitability.
- **Regional Performance:** Compare sales, profit, customers, and orders across cities, states, and regions.
- **Order Performance:** Examine order volume, average order value, order channels, payment methods, and purchasing patterns.
- **Time-Based Performance:** Explore changes in sales and revenue across months, quarters, years, and days of the week.
- **Business Improvement:** Identify potential opportunities to improve sales, profitability, and customer engagement.

## 5. Stakeholders

The analysis is intended to support the information needs of:

- CEO and Management
- Finance Manager
- Sales Manager
- Product Manager
- Marketing Manager
- Regional Manager

## 6. Key Business Questions

### Sales and Revenue
- What is the total revenue generated?
- How do sales and revenue change over time?
- Which products and categories generate the highest sales?
- Which locations contribute the most revenue?

### Profitability
- What is the total profit and overall profit margin?
- Which products and categories contribute the most profit?
- Which locations contribute the most profit?

### Customer Analysis
- How many unique customers does the business have?
- Which customers and segments contribute the most revenue and profit?
- What proportion of customers place repeat orders?
- How does customer contribution vary across gender and age groups?

### Product and Order Analysis
- Which products underperform in sales or profitability?
- How does Average Order Value vary across categories and locations?
- How do order channels and payment methods relate to purchasing patterns?

## 7. Key Performance Indicators

The business requirements identify the following KPIs:

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Profit Margin %
- Revenue Growth %
- Total Units Sold
- Average Selling Price
- Repeat Customer %
- Customer Revenue Contribution
- Customer Profit Contribution

## 8. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a structured, data-driven understanding of retail performance. The analysis should help management compare products, categories, customers, locations, and time periods, identify performance gaps, and investigate opportunities for business improvement.

## 9. Deliverable

A documented set of business requirements and analytical questions to guide the subsequent data understanding, cleaning, transformation, and analysis stages.
